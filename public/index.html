Handoff

Client content in. Client approvals out. All through Buffer.

Connect your Buffer account

Handoff uses your Buffer account to receive your clients' content as Ideas and to keep their approvals on your posts.

Sign in with Buffer

How it works: give each client their private link. What they send appears in Buffer as an Idea. Turn it into a draft in Buffer, and Handoff shows it to them to approve. For a draft you wrote yourself, use Share a draft on their card.

Handoff is an independent app built on the Buffer API. Not an official Buffer product. Privacy
